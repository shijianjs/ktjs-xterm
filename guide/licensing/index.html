Licensing

The wrapper​

ktjs-xterm is MIT licensed. See LICENSE.

Upstream​

@xterm/xterm and every addon wrapped here are MIT licensed:

  • @xterm/xterm 6.0.0 — MIT, © The xterm.js authors
  • @xterm/addon-fit 0.11.0, @xterm/addon-search 0.16.0, @xterm/addon-serialize 0.14.0, @xterm/addon-unicode11 0.9.0, @xterm/addon-web-links 0.12.0 — MIT, same holder

Their terms travel with their own packages, which remain dependencies of this module; nothing here re-licenses them.

What a consumer must do​

QuestionAnswer
May I ship this in a closed-source product?Yes — MIT permits it. Keep the upstream copyright notice, which the bundled npm packages carry.
Must I publish my source?No.
Are the addons a separate licence?Only nominally: each is MIT too. No wrapped part has a commercial edition.

The upstream repository also publishes @xterm/headless and website tooling that this wrapper does not touch; their licences are not part of this module.
